Short answer: the only way to get ChatGPT Business for less than OpenAI's list price is through an authorized OpenAI SMB referral partner. This replaces the earlier partner promotion, which gave one free seat for 48 months. Customers who already redeemed the old one-free-seat offer keep it for the rest of their original 48-month period.

The 30-second version: 20% off, up to 5 Standard seats, 48 months, through an authorized partner link. On monthly billing that is $5 off each of your first five seats: a 4-person team pays $80 instead of $100 a month; a 6-person team pays $125 instead of $150. Over 48 months the maximum saving is $1,200.

What the Promotion Covers

OpenAI's own examples make the mechanics clear:

  • Discount: 20% off the list price of a ChatGPT Business Standard seat.
  • Cap: applies to a maximum of five seats per workspace. The sixth seat onward is full price.
  • Duration: 48 months from redemption.
  • Seat type: Standard seats. OpenAI's examples do not mention Premium seats, so assume Premium is excluded unless OpenAI's checkout says otherwise.
  • How it's applied: through the partner's referral link. There is no code to type and nothing to negotiate.

OpenAI's examples use the $25 monthly Standard-seat price. If you choose annual billing, confirm the discounted rate at checkout before you commit; we will update this post once we have seen the annual math in a live workspace.

  1. Pick an authorized OpenAI SMB referral partner. Sayfe.ai is one. So are others; the promotion is the same program-wide, so compare partners on what they add around the subscription.
  2. Use the partner's referral link to create your workspace. The link lands on chatgpt.com with the promotion attached. Do not create the workspace direct first and try to apply the offer afterward; the discount is tied to the referral at signup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a ChatGPT Business promo code? ▼

Not one you type in. OpenAI's 20% partner promotion is attached to an authorized partner's referral link and applied at signup. Any site offering a typed ChatGPT Business coupon code is not part of OpenAI's SMB partner program.

How much is 20% off ChatGPT Business? ▼

$5 per seat per month at the $25 monthly Standard-seat price, on up to five seats. A 4-seat workspace pays $80 instead of $100; a 6-seat workspace pays $125 instead of $150. The discount runs for 48 months.

Does the discount apply to more than five seats? ▼

No. OpenAI's example is explicit: a 6-seat workspace gets 20% off five seats and pays the regular price for the sixth. Larger teams still save $25 a month, but the percentage shrinks as the team grows.

What happened to the one free seat for 48 months offer? ▼

OpenAI retired it in November 2026 and replaced it with 20% off up to five Standard seats for 48 months. Customers who already redeemed the free-seat offer keep it for the remainder of their original 48-month period.

Can I get the promotion if I already have a ChatGPT Business workspace? ▼

The promotion is applied through the referral link at workspace signup. If you already pay OpenAI direct, ask your partner; eligibility for existing workspaces is decided by OpenAI, not the partner, and we will not promise something OpenAI's checkout does not confirm.
