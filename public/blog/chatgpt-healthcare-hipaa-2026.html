Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChatGPT now HIPAA compliant?

Not automatically. Standard ChatGPT (Free and Plus) is not HIPAA-compliant. OpenAI's dedicated ChatGPT for Healthcare tier enables HIPAA-compliant use—but only when your practice signs OpenAI's Business Associate Agreement (BAA), configures the product correctly, and trains staff on compliant use. With that in place, patient data is encrypted and never used to train OpenAI's models.

What does OpenAI offer for HIPAA?

OpenAI offers a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) to ChatGPT for Healthcare and API healthcare customers. OpenAI says it encrypts business data with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit, and does not train on business data by default. A BAA is the legal step HIPAA requires; OpenAI also notes that a BAA does not by itself make your use HIPAA compliant.

Can I use regular ChatGPT Business for patient data?

No. OpenAI's help center says: "We do not offer a BAA for ChatGPT Business." ChatGPT Business does not train on your data by default and is a good fit for work that contains no protected health information. For PHI, use an offering OpenAI will sign a BAA for, such as ChatGPT for Healthcare, which is a sales-led contracted offering.

How are healthcare systems like Cedars-Sinai using ChatGPT?

Leading hospitals are using ChatGPT for clinical documentation, treatment research, patient communication, administrative workflows, and educational training. Adoption is accelerating across all practice sizes.
